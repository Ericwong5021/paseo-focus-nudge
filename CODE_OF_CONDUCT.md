# Community code of conduct

Treat people with respect across issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces. Disagree with ideas and code without attacking the person. Welcome questions, give actionable feedback, and respect different experience levels, languages, and backgrounds.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, deliberate intimidation, sexual harassment, doxxing, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Repeated disruption and spam may be removed.

Report a concern using GitHub's private reporting controls or by contacting the repository owner through a contact method they have publicly provided. Do not post private details in a public issue. Maintainers will handle reports as privately as practical, consider the circumstances, and may remove content, warn contributors, restrict participation, or block an account. Maintainers are subject to the same expectations.
